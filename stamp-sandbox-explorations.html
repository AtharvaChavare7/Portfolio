Stamp sandbox reveal explorations

Six ways the isometric sandbox cube can be generated inside the pink stamp. Click replay on any card to see it again.

01 — Stamp press

Frame drops like a rubber stamp; the cube prints into place on impact.

02 — Iris reveal

A circular aperture opens from the center, exposing the sandbox icon.

03 — Extrude rise

The cube rises from the bottom edge, like material being pushed up into form.

04 — 3D flip-in

The icon rotates into its isometric angle with a perspective flip.

05 — Ink bloom

Soft blur and overscale snap into a crisp, locked icon — like ink settling.

06 — Scan wipe
